SAFETY POLICY

We prioritise safety at every step — Zero accidents, continual improvement and training to protect our people and partners.


SAFETY POLICY

  • Safety First and Zero Accident Site is our Motto. We comply with the Occupational Health & Safety Act, Act 85 of 1993 and other relevant Legislation and Regulations.
  • Providing agreed levels of quality and service to our clients by appreciating and fully understanding their needs and expectations; identifying all Safety and Health hazards by compiling Risk assessments.
  • Establishing procedures to mitigate, reduce or eliminate identified risks to people, equipment, materials, environment and the community; Establishing effective management and maintenance of a Health, Safety, Environmental and Quality program.
  • Continuously improve our policies and systems and review them when needed, but at least once a year.
  • Reduce incidents and operational costs.
  • Inform all employees on their scope of authority.
  • Provide training, information, continual improvement and instructions concerning Health, Safety, Environmental and Quality for the benefit of all stakeholders.
  • Provide, as far as reasonably practicable, a safe environment for all, and to do everything possible to prevent injuries, occupational diseases, losses or pollution.
